Farmer Guide

List crops, reach buyers nationwide, and manage your store from one dashboard.

1. Register

Create a free farmer account with your farm name and province.

2. List crops

Add photos, ZMW prices, units, and stock from the dashboard. Active listings appear on the public shop.

3. Get paid

Buyers checkout online; payouts to Mobile Money are simulated in this demo.

Pricing & fees

  • Registration: Free
  • Listing crops: Free
  • Platform commission: 5% on completed orders (shown at buyer checkout)
  • Delivery: Coordinated separately; Lusaka deliveries typically ZMW 25 in demo checkout
