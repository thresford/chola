Our Story

Built for Zambia's Farmers & Families

We started with a simple belief — that every Zambian farmer deserves fair prices, and every family deserves fresh, affordable food. Online Crop Market is our answer to that challenge.

Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower Zambian smallholder farmers by giving them direct access to a nationwide market — eliminating middlemen, increasing income, and ensuring fresh produce reaches every household at fair prices.

Our Vision

A Zambia where no farmer struggles to sell their harvest, and no family struggles to afford nutritious, locally grown food. A fully connected agricultural ecosystem from field to fork.

Journey

How We Got Here

From a small idea to Zambia's fastest-growing agri-marketplace.

2021 — The Idea

Born from a Real Problem

Our founders noticed Lusaka farmers were selling maize below cost while market prices remained high. The disconnect was clear — farmers had no direct access to buyers. A digital marketplace was the obvious solution.

2022 — Launch

We Launched with 50 Farmers

After months of building, testing, and visiting farms across four provinces, we launched CropMarket with 50 verified farmers and 200 initial customers. Within three months, sales exceeded ZMW 250,000.

2023 — Growth

Expanding to All 10 Provinces

Word spread fast. By end of 2023, we had onboarded over 800 farmers from all 10 provinces, introduced mobile money payments, and launched the farmer dashboard enabling self-service listings.

2024–2026 — Today

2,400+ Farmers & Counting

Today, over 2,400 verified farmers use CropMarket to reach 48,000+ customers. We've facilitated over ZMW 18 million in transactions and helped farmers increase average monthly income by 62%.

Our Core Values

Everything we build is guided by these principles.

Fairness

We believe farmers deserve fair prices for their labour. Our platform eliminates exploitative middlemen and ensures every sale benefits the grower directly.

Sustainability

We champion organic and sustainable farming practices, helping farmers adopt better techniques that protect Zambia's soils and waterways for generations to come.

Trust & Safety

Every farmer is verified, every transaction is secured, and every buyer is protected by our quality guarantee. Trust is the foundation of our marketplace.

Community

We're not just a platform — we're a community. From farmer cooperatives to buyer groups, we help people connect, share knowledge, and grow together.

Innovation

We continuously improve our tools, listening to both farmers and buyers to build features that genuinely make agricultural commerce simpler and smarter.

Excellence

We hold high standards across everything — produce quality, platform reliability, customer service, and farmer support. Good enough is never good enough.

Whether you grow crops or you eat food — you have a role to play in building a fairer food system for Zambia. Join us today.
